WakeMATE Companion can expose this computer to authenticated control from another paired device.

Important:

- Setup does not launch the companion or configure a different Windows user's account. Open WakeMATE from the Start menu after Setup closes.
- Network operation and remote control require your explicit approval.
- The firewall allows only HTTPS and discovery from the local subnet on Private networks.
- Pairing invitations expire. Each approved phone receives its own revocable credential and permissions.
- Full keyboard and mouse control gives broad access to the signed-in desktop. Approve it only for devices and people you trust.
- Use the tray's pause/revocation controls if a phone is lost or its access is no longer wanted.

By continuing, you acknowledge that you understand these risks and will configure the software appropriately.
